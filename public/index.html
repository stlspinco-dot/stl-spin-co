FAQ

Good to know

Can I book the same date as someone else?

No — we take one booking per day. If your date is already taken, the form will tell you right away and your request will be auto-declined.

What happens after I send a booking request?

It lands as Under Review. Miles reviews it by hand and, once approved, you’ll get a confirmation email from stlspinco@gmail.com.

How do I check on my booking later?

Use My bookings below — log in with the email address you booked with and you’ll see only your own bookings and their status.

How do I get a price?

Pricing is quoted personally. Send a booking request with your date, hours, and guest count, or email stlspinco@gmail.com.

Where do you work?

St. Louis and the surrounding area.
